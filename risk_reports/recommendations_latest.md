## Critical Uncertainties

_These uncertainties bound the confidence interval of the recommendations. Planning must be robust to each._

- SSP2-4.5 rate assumptions may underestimate acceleration if West Antarctic Ice Sheet instability progresses faster than IPCC AR6 median projections.
- VLM rates for Bangladesh and Vietnam are based on sparse GNSS networks; true subsidence may exceed modelled rates in intensively pumped agricultural zones.
- Compound event probability estimates assume stationarity of storm surge climatology; climate-driven intensification of tropical cyclones could increase return period frequencies.
- Political will and institutional capacity for managed retreat remain the primary implementation bottleneck, not technical or financial constraints.
